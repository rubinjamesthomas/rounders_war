How the game works

  1. Tap each card to reveal it.
  2. Use every card to make a number.
  3. Round your number to the selected place.
  4. If that station is vacant, claim it.
  5. Get 4 consecutive stations to win.

Strategy

  1. You can choose either order of the cards.
  2. Think about which rounding result helps you.
  3. An occupied station gives you nothing.
  4. Block Mr Thomas when you can.

Rounders War

The goal: be the first player to claim four consecutive stations on the number line.

Example: cards 4 and 0 can make 40 or 04. Both are valid because both cards have been used. They round to different stations.

Important: the app checks your construction, but it does not construct the number for you. Drag the cards or type the number yourself.

In 2 Player mode, pass the device to the next player between turns. In CPU mode, Mr Thomas plays automatically.
